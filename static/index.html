Before we start

  • RoboDad is an AI chatbot for ages 13 and up. It can be wrong, so double-check anything important.
  • To write replies, your messages are sent to an AI service. RoboDad doesn't keep your chats.
  • Don't share your full name, address, school, passwords or photos.
  • If you're ever in danger, call 911.

How RoboDad uses your information

RoboDad is for teens 13 and up

It isn't available for you yet. Ask a parent, guardian or teacher about apps made for your age.

What should RoboDad call you?

A first name or nickname is plenty. It stays on this device and is sent with your messages so RoboDad can use it.
