    Before we start

    • RoboDad is an AI chatbot for ages 13 and up. It can be wrong, so double-check anything important.
    • To write replies, your messages are sent to an AI service. RoboDad doesn't keep your chats.
    • Don't share your full name, address, school, passwords or photos.
    • If you're ever in danger, call 911.
