Help test RoboDad

RoboDad is getting ready for the Google Play Store, and you can help get him there.

Why your help matters

Before a new app can go public, Google requires at least 12 people to test it for 14 days in a row. It takes about 10 minutes of your time in total.

…testers signed up so far

What you'll do

  1. Sign up below with the Gmail address on your Android phone.
  2. When testing opens, you'll get an email with a link to join.
  3. Tap the link, then install RoboDad from the Play Store like any other app.
  4. Open it a few times over the next 14 days and try a couple of questions. Tap 👍, 👎 or Report under replies; that's the feedback that makes RoboDad better.
  5. Stay in the test until the 14 days are up. That's it!

Count me in

Use the Google account that's signed in on your Android phone's Play Store.

Know someone with an Android phone?

Scan the code or share robodad.onrender.com/testers. Every tester gets RoboDad closer to the Play Store.

Your email is used only to invite you to RoboDad's Google Play test. It's kept in a private database and deleted once testing is finished. Read the privacy policy, or try RoboDad in your browser right now.
