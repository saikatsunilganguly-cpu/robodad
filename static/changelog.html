What's new

RoboDad started life as a college chatbot that ran in a terminal window. Here's everything that's changed since then, newest first.

  1. Version 1.15 · October 2026Latest

    Talk and thought bubbles

    • Voice and music now live in two comic bubbles next to RoboDad: a speech bubble for his voice and a thought bubble for music.
    • Tap a bubble to turn it on or off. A small note pops up with what's playing or which voice is on, and a Change button for picking another.
    • While music plays, the thought bubble shows your look's own player (CD, jukebox, skateboard, boombox and more) with notes floating out. The speech bubble's dots bounce while RoboDad reads a reply.
    • RoboDad sometimes says a word in his speech bubble when he does a move, like "GG!", "Rad!" or "Beep!".
    • The Voice and Music buttons above the message box are gone, so the chat screen is cleaner.
    • Fixed: updates now show up the next time you open RoboDad.
  2. Version 1.14

    Three new looks, each with its own tune

    • Skate Park '99: concrete, grip tape, a quarter-pipe and a "RAD DAD" sticker, with Skater Dad (beanie, hoodie) and an original pop-punk station, Half-Pipe '99. The music player is a skateboard with spinning wheels.
    • Block Party '03: summer sun, pool waves and jersey lettering, with Bucket Hat Dad and a laid-back boom-bap station, Block Party '03. The music player is a boombox.
    • Game Night '05: a dark gaming-lobby screen with a radar sweep and "PLAYER 1 · READY", with Gamer Dad (headset and mic) and a pumping electronic station, Respawn Lounge. The music player is a handheld console with a live equaliser.
    • Picking one of these looks (or Arcade Neon or Diner '55) switches the radio to that look's station.
    • The Y2K Gloss look now has Trucker Hat Dad: trucker cap, graphic tee and white earbuds. He tips his cap, bops to his music and flashes a peace sign.
    • New moves: Skater Dad pulls his beanie over his eyes, ollies a skateboard and gives you a fist bump. Gamer Dad says "GG!", button-mashes and pumps a victory fist. Bucket Hat Dad tips his hat, flashes "#1 DAD" on his jersey and gives a cool nod.
  3. Version 1.13

    A RoboDad for every decade

    • Five avatars, one for each look: Modern Dad in a polo (Classic), Arcade Dad with a sweatband and headphones (Arcade Neon), Video Store Dad in a backwards cap and flannel (VHS Sunset), Y2K Dad (Y2K Gloss), and the original Diner Dad in his leather jacket (Diner '55).
    • Every avatar has his own moves. Diner Dad pops his collar, shines his shades and combs his hair. Modern Dad waves, winks and sips his coffee. Arcade Dad head-bobs, drops his shades and scores a 1UP. Video Store Dad flips his cap and checks his pager.
    • He does a move every few minutes, and you can tap him any time to see one.
    • Pick any avatar in the ☰ menu, or let him match your look.
  4. Version 1.12

    His own voice, and quick controls

    • RoboDad now reads replies in his own deep, calm voice, and it sounds the same on every phone.
    • Pick from four voices: Troy (natural), Bruce (deeper), Peter (easygoing) or Austin (easygoing and deeper). If the online voice is unavailable, RoboDad uses your device's own voice.
    • New Voice and Music buttons just above the message box, so you can switch the voice or the station without opening Settings.
    • This "What's new" page, linked from Settings and the bottom of the chat.
  5. Version 1.11

    Collectible badges

    • The Life Skills Belt plates are now 12 collectible badges in four rarities: Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary.
    • Five secret bonus badges unlock when you complete a set, including the Legendary Undisputed badge for collecting all 12.
    • Unlocking a badge plays a reveal with light rays, confetti and a chime.
    • Share any badge you've earned as an image for your story or group chat.
    • A few names changed: Ride or Die is now Got Your Back, Kitchen Boss is Head Chef, and the Y2K Aero look is Y2K Gloss.
  6. Version 1.10

    Help test RoboDad, plus little touches

    • A Help test RoboDad page, with a QR code, for people who want to help get RoboDad onto the Google Play Store.
    • RoboDad nods when you send a message, new belt plates get a gold shine, and a ring around his face counts down the focus timer.
    • Replies no longer start with a stiff "I'm sorry, but I can't help with that."
    • Fixed: pages like Privacy now open properly when RoboDad is installed on your phone.
  7. Version 1.9

    Dad Mode

    • Trick questions, trolling and "what happens if…" messages now get a real dad answer instead of a flat "Sorry, I can't help with that."
    • RoboDad still won't help anyone get hurt, but he'll explain why and point you somewhere useful.
    • He handles busy moments better, so you get an AI answer more often instead of a basic one.
    • He won't write out song lyrics or recite movie lines, and he won't pretend to be a real person.
  8. Version 1.8

    Music players for every look

    • The music button at the top is now an animated player that matches your look: a smart speaker, a spinning CD, a cassette player, an MP3 player or a jukebox.
    • The music is easier to find and hear, including on iPhone.
  9. Version 1.7

    Diner '55

    • A new 1950s diner look, with chrome, a checkerboard trim, neon lettering, a tail-finned cruiser, a shake, a burger and fries, and a spinning 45 while RoboDad thinks.
    • A new doo-wop radio station, Jukebox '55, and Jukebox Pick song suggestions in the Diner look.
  10. Version 1.6

    RoboDad Radio and the dashboard

    • RoboDad Radio: relaxing stations made live on your device, with no ads and no downloads. The music dips while RoboDad talks.
    • A dashboard with the radio, a focus timer, a countdown, the dad joke and tip of the day, your belt and a help card.
    • A new look for RoboDad himself: slicked-back hair, aviators and a leather jacket over a white shirt.
  11. Version 1.5

    Retro looks and the Life Skills Belt

    • Pick a look: Classic, Arcade Neon, VHS Sunset or Y2K.
    • The Life Skills Belt: earn plates for real-life skills, like cooking a meal or making a budget.
    • Dad's Mixtape song picks when you're having a rough day, and "Ask your real one" questions to take to a parent.
    • Rate RoboDad's dad jokes with the groan buttons.
  12. Version 1.4

    Made for teens

    • Advice for teen life: first jobs, life after high school, friendships, healthy relationships, peer pressure, budgeting and getting around.
    • A quick age check, an optional nickname and a plain-language privacy page.
    • If something serious comes up, RoboDad shows real help right away, with tap-to-call numbers for Kids Help Phone and others.
    • A Report button on every reply.
  13. Version 1.3

    Smarter, and still free

    • RoboDad understands what you mean more often.
    • He remembers the conversation so far, so you don't have to repeat yourself.
    • Talk instead of typing, and have replies read aloud.
    • Install RoboDad on your home screen like an app.
    • Thumbs up and down on replies, so RoboDad can get better.
  14. Version 1.2

    RoboDad goes online

    • RoboDad moved from a terminal window to a website that works on phones and computers.
    • Real AI replies that appear word by word, written in RoboDad's voice.
    • If the free server is asleep, you'll see a "waking up" message instead of a blank screen.
  15. Version 1.0

    Where it started

    • RoboDad began as a CONL725 coursework chatbot: it worked out the topic of your message, picked a ready-made dad answer and ran in a terminal.
